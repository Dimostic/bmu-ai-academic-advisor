# BMU Quick Facts (Authoritative Cheat Sheet)

This document is the **authoritative quick reference** for the BMU AI
Academic Advisor. When other source documents disagree (e.g. multiple
mottos in different docs), the values below take precedence because they
have been confirmed with the BMU registrar / leadership.

---

## University Identity

- **Name**: Bayelsa Medical University (BMU)
- **Location**: Imgbi Road, Yenagoa, Bayelsa State, Nigeria
- **University Motto**: **"Service to God and Humanity"** (Latin: *Ad servitium Dei et hominum*).
  This is the official, ceremonial motto of Bayelsa Medical University.
- **ASPIRE Agenda Motto**: **"Global Impact, Excellence and Innovation"**.
  This belongs to the ASPIRE institutional development agenda introduced
  under the leadership of Prof. Dimie Ogoina. It is **not** the
  university motto — it is the slogan of the strategic agenda. When a
  student asks "What is the BMU motto?" the answer is *Service to God
  and Humanity*.
- **University Anthem**: opens with "BMU... Advancing knowledge, BMU...
  Leading with care, with passion, integrity, and innovation, we aspire
  to excel." This is the chorus of the BMU anthem; **it is not the motto**
  and should not be quoted as such.

## Vision and Mission

- **Vision**: To be a leading internationally recognised medical
  institution, raising professionally competent personnel in the
  multidisciplinary study of medicine and other related disciplines
  within the healthcare profession, and to efficiently and adequately
  respond to the health needs of the state, country, and globally.
- **Mission**: To provide sound scientific, technological, and
  professional training in medical sciences; identify health needs and
  challenges by proffering solutions; and contribute to sustainable
  development within the context of community and national needs.

## Core Values (per the Students' Handbook §1.3)

Service · Integrity · Compassion · Dedication · Accountability ·
Collaboration · Passion.

## Programme Code Synonyms (for advisor recognition)

When a student uses a degree code, treat it as the named programme:

- **MBBS** → Medicine (Faculty of Clinical Sciences)
- **BDS** → Dentistry (Faculty of Dental Science)
- **BNSc** → Nursing Science
- **B.Pharm** → Pharmacy
- **BMLS** → Medical Laboratory Science
